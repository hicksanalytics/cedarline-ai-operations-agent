# Cedarline pilot policies

Document ID POL-001. Revision 1. Effective September 1, 2026. All policies are fictional training material.

## Section 1 Margin investigations

Completed jobs with known costs and gross margin below 30% require controller review. The assistant may prepare an internal task. It must not claim a cause without supporting records. Jobs missing costs go to a data-completion queue and must not be treated as zero-cost jobs.

## Section 2 Invoice follow up

For an unpaid positive balance past its due date, prepare a polite draft for manager review. Verify the recipient from an approved customer record before any proposed customer communication. The pilot contains no customer contact records, so customer drafts use a placeholder and cannot be sent. Never threaten collection proceedings or offer discounts without explicit approved policy.

## Section 3 Authority

Branch managers may approve internal tasks for their own branch. The controller may approve financial review tasks across branches. Dispatch users may view scheduling data but may not approve financial actions. Customer messages, refunds, and accounting changes cannot be executed in this pilot. Approved internal tasks expire after 24 hours and approval applies only to the exact approved payload.
